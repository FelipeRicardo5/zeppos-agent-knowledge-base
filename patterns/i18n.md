# i18n

This article shares some experience in internationalization during the development of Zepp OS.

**Minimum API_LEVEL: >= 2.** The highest minimum among the 1 symbols this pattern's code uses — every one of them has to be available.

Source: `zeppos-docs/docs/guides/best-practice/i18n.mdx`

## Symbols used

| Symbol | Min API_LEVEL | Runtimes | In this KB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `@zos/i18n.getText` | >= 2 | Device App | yes |
